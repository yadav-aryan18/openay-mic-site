How it works

Three steps. About two minutes.

Step 1Install the app on your phone

Download the app below and open it. Allow the microphone permission when asked.

Step 2Run the receiver on your computer

Start OpenAY Mic on your Windows PC or Linux machine and leave it listening.

Step 3Connect and go live

Use the same Wi-Fi network, or plug in a USB cable. Press the big button. Done.

FAQ

Before you download

Is it really free? +
Yes. Open source under the MIT license, and the downloads are free. There is no paid tier, no account, and nothing is metered.
Do I need to "root" my phone or install drivers? +
No. Everything runs normally on both your phone and your computer — no special permissions beyond the microphone.
Will my apps actually see it as a microphone? +
On Linux, yes — everywhere. It appears as a regular microphone for every app. On Windows, you pair it once with a free "virtual audio cable" app (like VB-Cable) so other programs can select it — the same one-time setup streamers already use.
Wi-Fi or cable — which should I use? +
Try Wi-Fi first — it's the most convenient. If your network is busy and you hear hiccups, a USB cable gives a rock-solid connection.
Is my audio private? +
Your audio travels only between your phone and your computer on your own network. It never touches the internet, and the app collects nothing.
How current are the files on this page? +
The list refreshes automatically whenever a new version is published. The official releases page on GitHub is always the source of truth.
