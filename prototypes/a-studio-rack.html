The phone is the microphone.

OpenAY Mic streams your Android phone's audio to the desktop as a real, system-wide microphone — over Wi‑Fi or USB, raw PCM or Opus, with a measured p50 ≈ 47 ms end-to-end. No cloud in the path, ever.

WHAT THE LINK DOES
  • Transport Wi-Fi or USB — pick per session. UDP drops late packets instead of delaying them; TCP over adb never loses one. Bluetooth (SPP) is on the way.
  • Codec Raw PCM or Opus at 10 ms frames. 16-bit LE mono 48 kHz, or OPUS_APPLICATION_RESTRICTED_LOWDELAY. The wire protocol is frozen at v1.
  • Latency p50 ≈ 47 ms end-to-end, measured — not claimed. Adaptive jitter: +2 ms per underrun to a 20 ms ceiling, −1 ms per quiet minute. ~0.5 %CPU with Opus active.
  • Privacy Your audio never leaves the local network. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. The receiver latches to the first peer it accepts and ignores the rest.
  • Free MIT licensed. Downloads are free, forever. Wire spec and golden test vectors are published in the repo. Fonts ship under the SIL OFL.

Setup

THREE STEPS, NO ROOT
  1. 01Install the APK

    Side-load the phone app and allow the microphone permission.

  2. 02Run the receiver

    Start openay-gui on Linux or Windows and leave it listening on the LAN.

  3. 03Connect

    Same Wi-Fi network, or plug in USB. Press ON AIR on the phone.
