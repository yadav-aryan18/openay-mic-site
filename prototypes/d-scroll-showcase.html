FAQ

Quick answers

Is it really free? +
Yes. Open source under the MIT license — no paid tier, no account, nothing metered.
Do I need to root my phone or install drivers? +
No. It installs and runs like any normal app on both your phone and your computer.
Will my apps see it as a real microphone? +
On Linux, yes — everywhere. On Windows, you pair it once with a free "virtual audio cable" app (like VB-Cable) — the same one-time setup streamers already use.
